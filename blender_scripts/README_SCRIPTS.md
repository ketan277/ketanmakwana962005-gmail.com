# Golden & Crooked: Blender Scene Scripts

This folder contains Python scripts to automatically generate 14 cinematic scenes from the story "Golden, Crooked, Freed".

## How to use
1. Open **Blender**.
2. Go to the **Scripting** tab at the top.
3. Click **Open** and select one of the `.py` files from this folder.
4. Click **Run Script**.

## Scene List
- `scene_01_jungle.py`: The red-shaded jungle with the rock pond.
- `scene_02_mystic_reed.py`: The sailing boat on the open ocean.
- `scene_03_desert_oasis.py`: The sand dunes and palm tree pond.
- `scene_04_forgotten_island.py`: The island with blue sand and the vine-covered hut.
- `scene_05_red_cave.py`: The interior of the red stone cave with the gold-trimmed chest.
- `scene_06_towering_tree.py`: The exterior of Willow's giant tree with glowing roots.
- `scene_07_lake_observatory.py`: The silver dome in the lake with the massive telescope.
- `scene_08_starlight_tavern.py`: The interior of the dark, atmospheric tavern.
- `scene_09_unicorn_valley.py`: The lush green valley on Marsan Island.
- `scene_10_hot_air_balloon.py`: The patchwork balloon floating in the starry sky.
- `scene_11_waterfall.py`: The silvery glowing waterfall with gold-flecked mud.
- `scene_12_willow_studio.py`: The interior of Willow's home with the slanted desk and paintings.
- `scene_13_ruins.py`: The ancient stone ruins with a glowing phoenix footprint.
- `scene_14_mansion_zoo.py`: The grand hallway of Charles Bucket's mansion with creature pens.

## Features
- **Cinematic Lighting**: Each scene is pre-lit with specific light sources (Sun, Point, Area) to match the story's mood.
- **Ray-Tracing**: Configured for the **Cycles** engine for high-quality CGI results.
- **Procedural Materials**: Uses Blender's Principled BSDF for realistic wood, metal, water, and sand.
